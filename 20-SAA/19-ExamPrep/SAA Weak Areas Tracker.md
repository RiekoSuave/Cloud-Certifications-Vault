# Priority System

## 🔴 High Priority

Use when:

- Missed repeatedly
- Confidence is 1–2
- Cannot explain the concept
- Confuse similar services
- Same mistake appears across multiple exams

### Action

**Study before the next full practice exam**

---

## 🟡 Medium Priority

Use when:

- Understand after review
- Confidence is around 3
- Occasionally confuse details
- Need more scenario practice

### Action

**Review during normal study**

---

## 🟢 Low Priority

Use when:

- Confidence is 4–5
- Mistake was isolated
- Can explain why every answer is right/wrong
- Correctly recognize scenarios

### Action

**Quick periodic review**

## Master Memory Trick

> [!tip] Weak Areas Master Memory Trick
> Think of practice exams as:
>
> **A radar system**
>
> Your score tells you:
>
> **HOW YOU DID**
>
> Your mistakes tell you:
>
> **WHERE THE ENEMY IS**
>
> One miss:
>
> **NOTICE IT**
>
> Two misses:
>
> **REVIEW IT**
>
> Three misses:
>
> **ATTACK IT**
>
> Then:
>
> **RE-TEST IT**

The goal is not:

> **"I reviewed databases."**

The goal is:

> **"I no longer confuse Multi-AZ with Read Replicas."**

Not:

> **"I reviewed networking."**

But:

> **"I can immediately distinguish Peering, Transit Gateway, and PrivateLink."**

So remember:

> **TRACK**
> → FIND WEAKNESS
>
> **REVIEW**
> → UNDERSTAND
>
> **COMPARE**
> → REMOVE CONFUSION
>
> **PRACTICE**
> → BUILD RECOGNITION
>
> **RE-TEST**
> → PROVE MASTERY
