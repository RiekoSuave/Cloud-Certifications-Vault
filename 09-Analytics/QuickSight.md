## Scenario Questions

A company needs to create interactive dashboards from business data.

→ QuickSight

---

Executives need visual dashboards showing company data.

→ QuickSight

---

A company needs a serverless business intelligence service.

→ QuickSight

---

A company wants to visualize data coming from Athena.

→ QuickSight

---

A company wants dashboards using data from RDS, Aurora, Redshift, or S3.

→ QuickSight

---

A company needs to query files directly in S3 using SQL.

→ Athena

NOT QuickSight

---

A company needs to process real-time streaming data.

→ Kinesis

NOT QuickSight

---

A company needs Hadoop or Spark for big data processing.

→ EMR

NOT QuickSight

---

## Don't Confuse These

QuickSight = Dashboards & Visualization

Athena = Query S3

EMR = Big Data Processing

Kinesis = Real-Time Streaming

Redshift = Data Warehouse
