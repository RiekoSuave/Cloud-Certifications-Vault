## What Problem Does It Solve?

Provides different levels of AWS technical support.

**AWS Support Plans** solve the need for technical assistance by providing different levels of support based on the importance and complexity of your AWS workloads.

Think:

More Critical Workload

↓

Higher Support Level

↓

More AWS Assistance

### Memory Trick

Support Plans = Different Levels of Help

## Scenario Questions

A customer needs basic AWS documentation, whitepapers, and support forums.

→ Basic Support

---

A customer needs access to the 7 core Trusted Advisor checks.

→ Basic Support

---

A company is running production workloads and needs 24x7 phone, web, and chat access to Cloud Support Engineers.

→ Business Support+

---

A company needs third-party software support for something such as an EC2 operating system.

→ Business Support+

---

A company has a business-critical workload and needs a designated Technical Account Manager.

→ Enterprise Support

---

A company needs a response of less than 15 minutes for a production-critical case.

→ Enterprise Support

---

A company operates mission-critical workloads and needs specialized AWS support personnel.

→ Unified Operations Support

---

A company needs application architecture guidance for mission-critical workloads.

→ Unified Operations Support

---

## Don't Confuse These

Basic = Basic AWS Resources + Core Trusted Advisor Checks

Business Support+ = Production + 24/7 Engineers

Enterprise = Business Critical + TAM

Unified Operations = Mission Critical + Specialized Team
