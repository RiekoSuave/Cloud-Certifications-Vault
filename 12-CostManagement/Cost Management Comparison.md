## EC2 Pricing Models

These are covered in detail in [[02-Compute/EC2]].

| Pricing Model | Best For | Shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-Demand | Short/unpredictable workloads | PAY AS YOU GO |
| Reserved Instances | Steady-state workloads | COMMIT FOR DISCOUNT |
| Spot Instances | Interruptible workloads | CHEAPEST |
| Savings Plans | Long-term usage commitment | FLEXIBLE DISCOUNT |

### Memory Trick

On-Demand = FLEXIBILITY

Reserved = COMMIT

Spot = CHEAP

Savings Plans = FLEXIBLE COMMITMENT
