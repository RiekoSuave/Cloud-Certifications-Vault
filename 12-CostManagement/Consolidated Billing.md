## What Problem Does It Solve?

Combines billing across multiple AWS accounts.

**Consolidated Billing** solves the administrative overhead of managing separate bills for multiple AWS accounts by combining them into one bill.

It can also combine usage across accounts to provide pricing benefits.

Think:

Multiple AWS Accounts

↓

Consolidated Billing

↓

ONE BILL

### Memory Trick

Consolidated Billing = One Bill, Many Accounts

---

## Type

Multi-Account Billing

## Reserved Instance Discount Sharing

Your course notes that the:

Management Account

can turn off:

Reserved Instance Discount Sharing

for an account in the AWS Organization.

For CCP, the bigger concept to remember is:

Reserved Instance discounts can be shared across accounts through consolidated billing.

→ Consolidated Billing
