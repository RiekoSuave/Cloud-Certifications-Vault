## What Problem Does It Solve?

Creates spending limits and alerts.

**AWS Budgets** solves the problem of unexpected AWS spending by warning you when costs or usage exceed — or are forecasted to exceed — limits you set.

Think:

Set Budget

↓

Monitor Spending

↓

Receive Warning

### Memory Trick

Budgets = Warning System

## What Is AWS Budgets?

AWS Budgets is used to:

MONITOR SPENDING LIMITS

and

SEND ALERTS

Spending Alert

↓

## Budget Alerts

You define a:

LIMIT

AWS Budgets monitors your:

COST / USAGE

and can notify you when that threshold is reached.

Think:

Budget Limit

↓

AWS Spending

↓

Threshold Reached

↓

ALERT

### Memory Trick

Budget = Limit + Warning

---

## Forecasted Spending

Your note also identifies an important concept:

Budgets can warn when spending is:

FORECASTED

to exceed the limit you set.

Think:

Not Over Budget Yet

↓

Projected to Go Over

↓

Budget Warning

### Memory Trick

Budgets = Warn Before Trouble

## Scenario Questions

A company wants to receive alerts when AWS spending becomes too high.

→ Budgets

---

A company wants a warning when costs exceed a limit it has set.

→ Budgets

---

A company wants to prevent unexpected AWS spending.

→ Budgets

---

A company wants to be warned when spending is forecasted to exceed its limit.

→ Budgets

---

A company wants to analyze last month's AWS bill.

→ [[Cost Explorer]]

NOT Budgets

---

A company wants to estimate AWS costs before deploying an architecture.

→ [[Pricing Calculator]]

NOT Budgets

---

A company wants one bill for multiple AWS accounts.

→ [[Consolidated Billing]]

NOT Budgets
