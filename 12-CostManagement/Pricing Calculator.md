## What Problem Does It Solve?

Estimates AWS costs before deploying resources.

**AWS Pricing Calculator** solves the "how much will this cost?" problem during the planning phase of a project.

Think:

Plan AWS Architecture

↓

Pricing Calculator

↓

ESTIMATE COST

↓

Deploy Later

## Don't Confuse These

[[Cost Explorer]] = Analyze Existing Costs

[[Budgets]] = Spending Alerts
